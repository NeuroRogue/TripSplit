College Project Demo & Viva Presentation Guide
8-Step Final Demo Flow (From PDF Page 6)
Step 1: User Login / Authentication
Register or switch user from the top navigation bar. Backed by /api/auth/login and /api/auth/register.
Step 2: Create a Trip with Dates & Destination
Create trip through modal. Validates start and end dates. Backed by POST /api/trips.
Step 3: Add At Least Two Members
Add members to the trip. Backed by POST /api/trips/{tripId}/members.
Step 4: Add Hotel / Food Activity
Create categories (Hotel, Food, Sightseeing, Travel, Shopping). Backed by POST /api/trips/{tripId}/activities.
Step 5: Record Expense with Deterministic Paise Split
Validates payer & participants belong to trip. Uses MoneyUtils.splitEqually ensuring zero paise loss (e.g. ₹1000 / 3 = 333.34 + 333.33 + 333.33). Transactional persistence in expenses and expense_shares.
Step 6: Show Computed Balances & Simplified Debts
Calculates net balance for all members and runs the greedy min-cash flow algorithm to show who owes whom. Backed by GET /api/trips/{tripId}/balances.
Step 7: Record a Settlement & Refresh Balance
Click "Settle Up" to record payment between debtor and creditor. Updates settlements table and recalibrates net balance to zero. Backed by POST /api/trips/{tripId}/settlements.
Step 8: Open Dashboard & Visual Charts
Interactive Chart.js category spend chart and overall group spend metrics. Backed by GET /api/dashboard/summary.
